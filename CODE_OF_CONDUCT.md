# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive across languages and experience levels. Welcome good-faith questions and corrections. Critique ideas and code, not people. No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential sharing or spam. Respect private data and third-party ownership.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation, applying proportionate measures and considering context. Report problems through the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/kongjiestak); a dedicated confidential reporting channel is not yet established. Do not post sensitive details in public issues. No private response-time or enforcement guarantee is implied.

中文：尊重不同语言和经验的贡献者，具体讨论代码，不攻击个人。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、曝光私人资料、传播凭据和垃圾信息。维护者可移除有害内容或限制参与。通过维护者 GitHub 页面联系；尚无专用保密渠道，不在公开 Issue 张贴敏感内容。
