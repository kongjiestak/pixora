# Pixora — Brand & Messaging Guide

**Brand status:** Working name / proposed public-facing brand. Not legally cleared or registered.

## Core

- **Name:** Pixora
- **Mission / Tagline:** Make Every Screen Useful.
- **Chinese tagline:** 让每一块屏幕都有价值。
- **Supporting line:** One Platform. Any Screen. Your Way.
- **Principle:** Your Screen. Your Data. Your Control.
- **Brand position:** Open-source, self-hostable, multilingual, cross-device personal information & dashboard platform.

## 中文项目介绍

Pixora 是一个开源、可自托管、支持多语言的个性化信息展示与交互平台。它让闲置手机、平板、桌面显示器和基于 ESP32 的 DIY 硬件重新发挥价值，成为属于用户自己的信息终端。

借助可扩展的数据连接器、模块化 Widget 和自由组合的仪表盘，Pixora 计划把 AI 服务使用状态、服务器监控、开发工作流、个人信息与智能家居整合到不同设备上。用户可以自主决定展示内容、部署位置与数据权限。

无需专用硬件，也不强制依赖某一家云服务。让每一块屏幕都有价值。

## English project introduction

Pixora is an open-source, self-hostable platform for turning everyday screens into personalized information hubs.

From old phones and tablets to desktop displays and DIY ESP32 devices, Pixora gives existing hardware a new purpose. Through modular widgets, customizable dashboards, and extensible data connectors, it aims to bring AI usage, system monitoring, developer workflows, personal information, and smart-home controls together — on your terms.

No dedicated hardware required. No mandatory cloud subscription. Your screen, your data, your control.

## Brand guidelines

- Prefer a wordmark while the brand is provisional. Avoid unlicensed logos, fonts, and copied images.
- Keep the mission consistent in all translations; do not auto-translate the Pixora name.
- Distinguish roadmap concepts from actual features.
- Do not imply affiliation with OpenAI, ESP32/Espressif, Home Assistant, or GitHub.
- Avoid calling Pixora a unique/registered trademark before legal clearance.
- Existing third-party names: GitHub `pixora` organization is occupied, and other `Pixora`-named services exist. Do independent trademark/domain checks before a major marketing launch.
